## 2026-11-22 — G05 guarded recovery backend

Schema 13 adds an import-time fingerprint of the complete QSO row, per-service QSL state, attachment metadata and enrichments. It is calculated and recorded within the QSO import savepoint. The durable `undoLatestImport` operation opens a transaction per journalled QSO, checks its local ID, persistent UUID, revision and fingerprint, and soft-deletes only unchanged records. Already-deleted, missing/replaced, changed and failed records are counted separately; a later retry does not repeat completed deletions. Schema-12 journal entries have no baseline and are conservatively skipped for review. A profile change or service update that modifies the stored row can produce a conservative skip.
